Halyard

WiFi print server for Marlin printers · firmware @VERSION@

1. Install the firmware

  1. Plug the Waveshare ESP32-S3-GEEK into this computer's USB port.
  2. Press Install and pick the GEEK's port. Nothing needs installing first.
  3. When it says the installation is complete, unplug the GEEK and plug it back in. Until then it stays in its installer and Halyard doesn't start.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer (not Firefox or Safari).

The installer only works over https or on localhost.
  • No port listed? Unplug the GEEK, hold its BOOT button while plugging it back in, then try again.
  • A first install should erase the device. Updating an installed Halyard is easier from its own update page, http://<halyard>/halyard/, and keeps its settings.
  • On Linux, your user must be in the dialout group to open the port.

2. Prepare the microSD card

  1. Use a card of 32 GB or less, formatted FAT32.
  2. Download halyard-card-@VERSION@.zip and unzip it onto the card.
  3. Open halyard.cfg on the card in a text editor and fill in ssid and password under [wifi].

3. Fit it to the printer

  1. Put the card in the GEEK and wire its UART connector to the printer's serial header, with power from the header's 5 V (never 3.3 V).
  2. Power on. The printer's screen shows Halyard 192.168.x.y, or what's wrong.
  3. Open that address for Mainsail. In the slicer, add a printer host of type OctoPrint at the same address.

Stuck? Plug the GEEK into the computer, press Install again and choose Logs & Console: Halyard's console lists its commands with help, and wifi shows or sets the network.
